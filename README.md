## Artai Piñeiro · Geospatial Specialist

Geographer with 16 months as a hydrographic surveyor, including offshore wind-farm projects, and a Master in GNSS. I turn raw spatial data into products people can measure, trust and reuse: documented sources, datums and limits on every deliverable.

**Focus:** bathymetry and seabed mapping · lidar terrain and contours · cartography and print-ready maps · GNSS and positioning QC · reproducible QGIS/GDAL workflows
